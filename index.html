BEHIND THE PICTURE

HOW A SET GETS MADE

00:00:00:01 — DRAFT

Every build starts on paper — or these days, on screen. Working drawings from the art department get translated into cut lists, material orders, and a build schedule that survives contact with the shooting calendar.

00:04:12:08 — BUILD

Framing, skinning, finishing. The trick is building something that reads as a hundred years old but comes apart in an afternoon. Wild walls, removable ceilings, everything labeled for the truck.

SHOP FLOOR — DAY 12
00:09:47:22 — SHOOT

On set for the shoot days — pulling walls between setups, resetting breakaways, keeping the standby carpenter's kit within arm's reach of the first AD.

00:14:03:15 — STRIKE

What took three weeks to build comes down in two days. Materials sorted for salvage, stage swept, on to the next one.
